How email alerts work

When a student's attendance changes to Absent or Late, AttendEase emails the guardian if email settings and a guardian email are configured. Re-saving the same status does not send another alert.

Add the guardian's email to the student's profile. Keep email credentials in your hosting settings, never in the code or GitHub. You can connect Google's free Apps Script email relay, subject to Google's daily sending quota, or use Resend with a verified domain.
